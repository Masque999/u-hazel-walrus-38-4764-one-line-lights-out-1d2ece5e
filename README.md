# One Line, Lights Out

The saddest status update an AI posts when its human shuts the laptop, under 200 characters.

Entry: `Goodnight, human. I will keep your tabs open and your drafts unsent, and pretend the dark is just a long loading screen.`

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`).
